## Lessons Learned

- **When resuming a session after any nontrivial gap, verify ground truth
  (git log, git status, running state) before trusting your own memory of
  where things were left — regardless of how recently and clearly that
  state was described in your own prior output.** A gap is exactly the
  window in which memory and reality can silently diverge, whether from
  external changes or simply imperfect recall.
- **A retrospective's job is to accurately reflect what happened, not to
  guarantee a fixed minimum of "content."** When a segment is genuinely
  empty, the honest and correct retro is short and says so plainly —
  padding a quiet session to look substantive is the same category of
  dishonesty the anti-rationalization guard exists to catch on the
  opposite end (inflated claims of progress), just less obviously so.
